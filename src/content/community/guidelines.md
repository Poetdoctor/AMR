This is a small space run by the iGEM UBC Human Practices team for people affected by antimicrobial-resistant infections: patients, the people who care for them, and anyone else who has lived close to it. It is moderated by the website administration. Please read this before you post. It is short.

## Community guidelines

**Share your own experience.** What happened, how it felt, what helped. That is what this space is for.

**Don't give medical advice.** Don't tell anyone to start, stop or change a treatment. Everyone's infection and history are different, so point people to a clinician instead.

**Be respectful.** No insults, harassment, bullying or hate, and no mocking anyone's illness, body, faith, culture or choices. Disagree kindly or not at all.

**Leave out personal information, yours and anyone else's.** That means real names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, social media handles, the name of a hospital, ward or clinician, exact dates, and photos. Small details add up: a ward, a town and a date together can identify somebody.

**No advertising, fundraising links or spam.**

**If you are in crisis, please don't wait for a reply here.** Call or text 9-8-8, the Suicide Crisis Helpline, any hour of any day, anywhere in Canada. For a worry about your health, call 8-1-1, HealthLink BC, to speak to a nurse.

## Terms of use

### Everything you post is public

Anything you post or comment here can be read by anyone on the internet, without signing in. We ask search engines not to list this section, but we cannot stop people copying or taking screenshots of what they read.

### You post under a made-up name

The first time you post, comment, react or join, the site gives you a username it has made up, like MapleGrove123. You cannot choose it or change it, and it is the only name anyone sees. Please don't put your real name in what you write either.

### What we keep, and what we don't

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any health records, and there is no account or profile about you. What we keep is what you choose to post: the text of your stories and comments, the tags and content warning you picked, your made-up name, your reactions and saved stories, and any reports you make.

Your browser keeps a random key that lets you delete what you wrote. If you clear your browser's data, or switch to another device, that key is gone, and you will need to ask us to remove things for you (see below).

The companies that host this site and its database keep routine technical records, such as IP addresses, to keep the service secure. We do not use them to find out who you are.

### Contact details are your choice, and your risk

We strongly advise you not to share your phone number, email address or any other contact details here. If you choose to share your own anyway, you do so having accepted these terms, and knowing that:

- they are visible to everyone who reads the page, not only to the person you meant them for;
- once posted, who sees them and what they do with them is out of our hands;
- we may remove them if they look like someone else's, or if sharing them looks unsafe.

Never post anyone else's contact details.

### How moderation works

Posts and comments appear as soon as you publish them. The website administration reviews everything new every day, and may hide, edit or remove anything that breaks these guidelines. Anything that two different people report is hidden until it has been reviewed.

### Removing something

- **Delete it yourself.** From the same browser you posted from, use **Delete** on your story or comment. It is deleted from our database, not just hidden.
- **Ask us through the site.** Use **Report** and choose **This is mine and I want it removed**. It is hidden straight away and removed at the next review.
- **Email us** at [humanpractices@ubcigem.com](mailto:humanpractices@ubcigem.com). Tell us the made-up name and roughly what the post said. Write to the same address if somebody has posted your details, or about anything else to do with this space.

### Not medical advice

Everything here is people's own experience. It is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan, and it does not replace a conversation with a healthcare provider.

### Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, the date at the top of this page changes too. The version that applies is the one shown here when you post.
