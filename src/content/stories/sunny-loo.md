> I want others to understand how I feel and stop long enough to allow me to share how I feel instead of going straight into...the treatment

## The pause that doesn’t happen

The request here is not for a different treatment. It is for the moment before
it — long enough to say how this is actually going, and to be heard saying it,
before the conversation moves on to the plan.

Clinicians we spoke to described the same gap from the other side. As physicians
specialise, they debrief patients on the basics less often, and the explaining
quietly stops being anybody’s job.

## What helped

Online gaming with friends — a connection that carried on regardless of what
their body was doing, and did not require them to be a patient in order to take
part. They told us the same thing the other account here does: a real community
for this does not currently exist.

> When we talk about our experience, we are just asking to be understood, to be supported.
